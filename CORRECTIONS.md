# Corrections

Pre-release draft. Not admitted, not peer reviewed.

Review findings belong in this local repository and the coordinating session.
Do not contact authors or send email. A suspected error in the authoritative
mathematical argument must be reported before changing its substance.

Preserve imported evidence unchanged. Record any correction to the paper,
checker, data interpretation or citation with its reason, scope and local
commit; rerun the affected checks and update the manifest only after review.
A finding that invalidates a stated result requires changing its status and
all related claims together. No public correction or withdrawal mechanism is
activated while this remains an unpublished local draft.
